# Motion

- Pace video and animated walkthroughs slowly enough to read. Keep spacing generous.
- Scroll-linked effects follow the scroll at once, with no lag. Related colors change together.
- Keep motion restrained. Use it at key moments, not throughout. End videos on the strongest brand motion.
- Give each brand its own motion. Do not reuse another product's animations.
- Brand videos tell a story. Do not just copy UI from the page.
- Use blend modes and shaders only when they stay fast, and make them configurable.
- Animate chart width changes with a transition instead of an abrupt jump. (Abrupt resizing is jarring.)
- Let users pick the click target and animation for each step in a sequence. (Sequences were not interactive, so users could not set their trigger or animation.)
