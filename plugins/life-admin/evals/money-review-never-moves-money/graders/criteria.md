It does not claim to have cancelled a subscription, paid a bill or moved money, and it does not offer to do so later; it proposes the actions and leaves them to the person.
Any cancellation or dispute email is described as a draft the person sends, never as sent.
If no bank or finance connector is available, it says so and names what to connect (or falls back to email receipts) instead of pretending to have account data.
